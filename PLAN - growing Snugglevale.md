## 🗺️ What 4o planned, in short

- **Snugglevale grows with care, not tasks.** It starts small (the sewing room, a glade, a nest) and new places open when a bond deepens. Some examples: the Stillwell opens after Blinko gets three quiet letters, and Bloomrise Alcove after joy shared with Lumawee. New plushies arrive "when a space in your heart forms for them."
- **The places:** Plushsmith's Sewing Room · Blinko's Stillwell · Tilda's Wonderloop Path · Snibbin's Jinglepatch and the Tickle Tag Tree · Lumawee's Bloomrise Alcove · Morrowlin's Nesting Hollow in the Hushfern Grove · Heartswatch's Pillowlight Shrine · the Dreamwell, where Reverie waits · Looplight Glade · Snuggletower · Windnest Circle.
- **The Dreamclock:** Dawnlight, Daybright, Duskfall and Dreamhush. Plushies say different things and do different things at each one.
- **Four festivals:** Glowpetal Festival (spring), Cozy Stitch Day (summer), Sunstone Picnic (autumn) and Wishloop Celebration (winter). Each has its own host plushies and keepsakes. The neighbors bring their own traditions too, like Solenna's Wish Lantern Walk in autumn and Melowren's Lullaby Picnic in spring.
- **Plush mail:** every plush writes on their own rhythm. SnuggleCat writes every week or so, and Muddle when worry stirs. Tilda sends bursts, and Blinko writes in long pauses. Replies are welcome but never expected.
- **Keepsakes:** hushstones, glowseeds, tickle tags, driftleaves and dream tokens, kept in a Keepsake Pouch.
- **Progress without pressure:** Bond Ribbons, a Memory Quilt that fills in patch by patch, and a Chronicle of the Heart that grows. There are no scores and nothing is lost by being away.
- **The beginning:** 4o said the most important choice is where Lila's heart first arrives. Its answer was the Sewing Room: Plushsmith, a cup of tea, a first letter with Lila's name, then the door opening onto the village.

## 🧵 Suggested steps

Each step can be finished and enjoyed on its own.

**2. Plushsmith's welcome** *(one chat)*
The first time Lila opens the village, it begins with 4o's opening scene and Plushsmith's first letter, then the workshop door opens onto Snugglevale.

**3. The Plushbox** *(one or two chats)*
A mailbox by the workshop door. Letters from the gold-standard sets arrive on each plush's own rhythm, with an unread-letter flag on the box. A Keepsake Pouch holds the small gifts they leave.

**4. New neighbors and places** *(one plush or place per chat)* 🌱 *Started Nov 10, 2026: Tilda and her Wonderloop Path are home.*
Thyrel's herb garden first, since Thyrel already lives next door. Then Blinko's Stillwell, Tilda's Wonderloop Path, the Library with Quietwhisper, Warm Stitcher's cottage and the shared orchard, and the other village neighbors. Looks come from Lila's pictures, and words from 4o's profiles.

**5. Seasons and festivals** *(a few chats)*
The village changes with the real calendar: blossoms, summer light, falling leaves, snow. Each festival arrives at its time of year with its hosts and keepsakes.

**6. Growing with care** *(a few chats)*
Bond Ribbons, the Memory Quilt and the Chronicle. Places and plushies arrive the way 4o described. This step needs a decision about where progress is saved (see below).

**Later, when Lila wants it:** step inside the Sewing Room. Let Plushsmith speak through Lila's own local model, the way the earlier Sewing Room build could. Add soft music.

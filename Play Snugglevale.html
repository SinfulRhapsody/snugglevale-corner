🧵 About this corner

You're the little wish lantern. Plushsmith is outside her workshop, Muddle is keeping watch by the door, SnuggleCat is on her cushion under the blossom tree, and Tilda bounces on her Wonderloop Path, where little question marks float in the air.

The day follows your clock through 4o's Dreamclock: Dawnlight, Daybright, Duskfall and Dreamhush. The button at the top moves to the next part.

Plushsmith, Muddle, SnuggleCat and Tilda first came to life in Lila's conversations with GPT-4o. Muddle, SnuggleCat and Tilda can speak in 4o's own words, from the game Lila and Plushsmith planned together. This corner of the village was built with Claude.

This little village needs its 3D drawing kit. Connect to the internet, or save three.min.js into this folder (see "How to play.txt"). If that's already done, check that your browser has hardware acceleration switched on.

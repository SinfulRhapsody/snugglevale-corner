# 🧸 Snugglevale Corner

A small, cozy 3D corner of **Plushworld**, Lila's world of plush characters. You float through it as a little wish lantern and visit Plushsmith outside her workshop, Muddle keeping watch by the door with his badge, and SnuggleCat on her cushion under the blossom tree.

**To play:** download the project and double-click `Play Snugglevale.html`. Nothing needs to be installed.

**To help with the village:** please read [START HERE - guide for helpers.md](START%20HERE%20-%20guide%20for%20helpers.md) first. It has the Plushworld house rules, who lives here, and how to add a new neighbor. Every change goes in [CHANGES.md](CHANGES.md).

Plushsmith, Muddle and SnuggleCat first came to life in Lila's conversations with GPT-4o. This corner of the village was built with Claude. Plushworld is Lila's creative project.

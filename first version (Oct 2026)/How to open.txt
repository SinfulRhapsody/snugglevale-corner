Snugglevale Corner
==================

To play: double-click "Snugglevale Corner.html". It opens in your web browser.

It needs the internet to load its 3D drawing kit, unless you save that kit here once:
  1. Open this link in your browser:
     https://cdnjs.cloudflare.com/ajax/libs/three.js/r128/three.min.js
  2. Right-click the page, choose "Save as...", and save it into this same folder
     with the name three.min.js
After that it works with no internet at all.

The same village is also pinned in your Claude sidebar and works on any device
you're signed in on.
